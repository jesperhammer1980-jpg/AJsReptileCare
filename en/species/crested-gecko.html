Detailed care sections

Read-more links now scroll to these visible sections.

Enclosure size

Minimum: 45 × 45 × 60 cm. Recommended: 45–60 × 45–60 × 90 cm.

Day/night heat

Day: 22–26°C. Night: 18–22°C. Basking/warm zone: Let varm zone 25–27°C, ikke kraftig solplads. Cool zone: 20–23°C.

Light, UVB and photoperiod

Photoperiod: 10–12 timer; mørk nat er vigtig. UVB: Lav UVB kan bruges; rigeligt løv/skygge skal være tilgængeligt.

Humidity

60–80% efter spray med udtørring mellem perioder.

Feeding

Komplet geckodiæt suppleret med passende insekter.

Calcium and vitamins

Brug komplet geckodiæt; calcium på insekter. Undgå ekstra D3 oven i stærk UVB uden plan.

Brumation / winter rest

Egentlig vinterdvale ikke relevant; let sæsonmæssig nedgang kan ske.

Breeding guidance

Kun voksne hunner i god vægt; æglægningsmedie og calciumkontrol er vigtigt.

Care, cleaning and supplement plan

Daily, weekly and seasonal routines for stable care.

Daily

Check water, temperatures at both ends, behaviour, waste and appetite.

Weekly

Feed according to age and species; follow calcium/vitamin rhythm; clean bowls and high-contact surfaces.

Monthly

Deep-clean selected areas, inspect heat/UVB equipment, weigh animals where relevant and review enclosure wear.

Seasonal

Adjust photoperiod carefully, evaluate night drops, breeding plans and brumation only for suitable species.
