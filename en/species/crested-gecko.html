Detailed care sections

Read-more links now scroll to these visible sections.

Enclosure size

Minimum: 18 × 18 × 24 in. Recommended: 45–60 × 45–24 × 35 in.

Day/night heat

Targets: 72–79°F, 64–72°F, 77–81°F, 68–73°F. Provide a measured warm zone, cool zone and safe night drop where appropriate.

Light, UVB and photoperiod

Photoperiod/UVB: 10–12 hours. Use species-appropriate UVB distance, bright daytime light and dark nights.

Humidity

60–80% after misting, with drying periods between.

Feeding

Feed an appropriate species-specific diet and adjust prey or portion size to age, size and body condition.

Calcium and vitamins

Use calcium, D3 and multivitamins according to diet, UVB exposure and species needs. Avoid overdosing.

Brumation / winter rest

Brumation or seasonal rest is species-specific and should only be planned with correct health checks, temperatures and experience.

Breeding guidance

Advanced only: use healthy adult animals, correct sexing, species-specific egg-laying provision and a plan for offspring.

Care, cleaning and supplement plan

Daily, weekly and seasonal routines for stable care.

Daily

Check water, temperatures at both ends, behaviour, waste and appetite.

Weekly

Feed according to age and species; follow calcium/vitamin rhythm; clean bowls and high-contact surfaces.

Monthly

Deep-clean selected areas, inspect heat/UVB equipment, weigh animals where relevant and review enclosure wear.

Seasonal

Adjust photoperiod carefully, evaluate night drops, breeding plans and brumation only for suitable species.
