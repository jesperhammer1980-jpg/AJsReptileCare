Enclosure size

Minimum: 30 × 30 × 45 cm for lille gruppe. Recommended: 45 × 45 × 60 cm eller større.

Day/night heat

Day: 23–27°C. Night: 20–23°C. Basking/warm zone: 26–28°C mild zone. Cool zone: 21–23°C.

Light, UVB and photoperiod

Photoperiod: 10–12 timer. UVB: Lav UVB valgfrit.

Humidity

60–80% med spray og ventilation.

Feeding

Små insekter + geckodiæt.

Calcium and vitamins

Calcium på insekter; brug komplet diæt.

Brumation / winter rest

Ingen.

Breeding guidance

Parthenogenetisk art; formerer sig let, planlæg plads til afkom.

Care, cleaning and supplement plan

This replaces the old static checklist with an actual routine.

Daily

Check water, temperatures at both ends, behaviour, waste and appetite.

Weekly

Feed according to age and species; follow calcium/vitamin rhythm; clean bowls and high-contact surfaces.

Monthly

Deep-clean selected areas, inspect heat/UVB equipment, weigh animals where relevant and review enclosure wear.

Seasonal

Adjust photoperiod carefully, evaluate night drops, breeding plans and brumation only for suitable species.

Terrarium inspiration

Use as inspiration for zones, cover and structure — always follow the species-specific values above.
