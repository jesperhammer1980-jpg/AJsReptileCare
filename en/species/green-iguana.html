Detailed care notes

Each quick panel above now links to the relevant explanation below, so the buttons do not just jump to the general checklist.

More detail: Heat

Use the panel temperatures as practical checkpoints. Measure both warm and cool zones with digital thermometers, and use thermostats on active heat sources so the animal can thermoregulate without overheating.

Quick requirement: Tropical heat with a large basking area; adults require large warm zones and stable temperatures.

Back to panel ↑

More detail: Light / UVB

Lighting and UVB must match the species, activity pattern and distance to the animal. Always provide shade and hiding areas so the reptile can regulate exposure throughout the day.

Quick requirement: Strong UVB and bright light are necessary for calcium metabolism and welfare.

Back to panel ↑

More detail: Humidity

Humidity should be controlled with a hygrometer and ventilation, not misting alone. Aim for the correct species range and provide humid hides or drying-out periods where the species requires it.

Quick requirement: High tropical humidity, often around 70–90%, combined with ventilation.

Back to panel ↑

More detail: Food

Feeding should follow the species’ natural diet, age and body condition. Avoid overfeeding, use supplements where relevant, and adjust amounts to growth, activity and body shape.

Quick requirement: Herbivore: leafy greens, herbs and vegetables; avoid animal protein.

Back to panel ↑

More detail: Enclosure

The enclosure should support natural behavior: hiding, climbing, digging, basking or swimming depending on the species. The setup should also make heat, humidity and cleaning easy to control.

Quick requirement: Very large, tall custom enclosure with branches, basking areas and bathing/misting options.

Back to panel ↑

More detail: Handling

Handling should be short, calm and adapted to the species’ temperament. Support the animal correctly, avoid grabbing tail or limbs, and stop if it shows clear stress or escape behavior.

Quick requirement: Can become large and powerful; requires experience, space and calm training.

Care checklist

Equipment checklist

Product cards and prices have been removed. Users should not see random recommendations until local and relevant products can be shown.

  • Correctly sized enclosure or habitat
  • Thermostat for all heat sources
  • Digital thermometer in warm and cool zones
  • Hygrometer for humidity control
  • UVB lighting where required or beneficial
  • At least two hides plus species-specific furnishing
  • Safe substrate with no known risk for the species
  • Water bowl, feeding dish or mist/drip system by species
  • Cleaning routine and quarantine plan for new animals

Source principle

This guide is a practical overview based on recognized welfare and reptile husbandry sources. Always check temperature, UVB distance, humidity and feeding against species, age, health and advice from a reptile-qualified veterinarian.
