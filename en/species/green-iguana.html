Care checklist

Equipment checklist

Product cards and prices have been removed. Users should not see random recommendations until local and relevant products can be shown.

  • Correctly sized enclosure or habitat
  • Thermostat for all heat sources
  • Digital thermometer in warm and cool zones
  • Hygrometer for humidity control
  • UVB lighting where required or beneficial
  • At least two hides plus species-specific furnishing
  • Safe substrate with no known risk for the species
  • Water bowl, feeding dish or mist/drip system by species
  • Cleaning routine and quarantine plan for new animals

Source principle

This guide is a practical overview based on recognized welfare and reptile husbandry sources. Always check temperature, UVB distance, humidity and feeding against species, age, health and advice from a reptile-qualified veterinarian.
