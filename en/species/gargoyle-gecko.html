Care dashboard

🔥

Heat

Thrives at moderate temperatures; keep around 22–26°C with a warm zone around 25–29°C and avoid overheating.

Read more
💡

Light / UVB

Low to moderate UVB can be beneficial even though the species is crepuscular/nocturnal; provide shaded hides.

Read more
💧

Humidity

Humid but well-ventilated; often 60–80% with a clear drying-out period between misting.

Read more
🍽

Food

Complete crested/gargoyle gecko diet as a staple, supplemented with suitable insects such as crickets and dubia roaches as needed.

Read more
🏠

Enclosure

Tall enclosure with cork, branches, plants and many elevated hiding areas; this species actively uses vertical space.

Read more
✋

Handling

Can jump and may drop the tail; handle low, calmly and briefly without grabbing the tail.

Care checklist

Equipment checklist

Product cards and prices have been removed. Users should not see random recommendations until local and relevant products can be shown.

  • Correctly sized enclosure or habitat
  • Thermostat for all heat sources
  • Digital thermometer in warm and cool zones
  • Hygrometer for humidity control
  • UVB lighting where required or beneficial
  • At least two hides plus species-specific furnishing
  • Safe substrate with no known risk for the species
  • Water bowl, feeding dish or mist/drip system by species
  • Cleaning routine and quarantine plan for new animals

Source principle

This guide is a practical overview based on recognized welfare and reptile husbandry sources. Always check temperature, UVB distance, humidity and feeding against species, age, health and advice from a reptile-qualified veterinarian.
