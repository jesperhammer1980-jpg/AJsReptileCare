Detailed care sections

Read-more links now scroll to these visible sections.

Enclosure size

Minimum: 60 × 60 × 120 cm. Recommended: 90 × 60 × 120+ cm med god ventilation.

Day/night heat

Day: 24–28°C generelt. Night: 16–20°C natfald er ofte gavnligt. Basking/warm zone: 30–35°C afhængigt af alder/køn. Cool zone: 22–24°C.

Light, UVB and photoperiod

Photoperiod: 12 timer stærkt lys; tydelig mørk nat. UVB: Lineær UVB med korrekt afstand og skyggegradient.

Humidity

50–70% med morgen/aften spray og god udtørring/ventilation.

Feeding

Varierede gutloadede insekter; hunner fodres kontrolleret for at mindske æglægningspres.

Calcium and vitamins

Calcium hyppigt; D3 og multivitamin sparsomt efter UVB-plan.

Brumation / winter rest

Ingen egentlig vinterdvale, men natfald og sæsonrytme er relevant.

Breeding guidance

Avl er risikabelt for hunner; kræver æglægningsbeholder selv uden parring.

Care, cleaning and supplement plan

This replaces the old static checklist with an actual routine.

Daily

Check water, temperatures at both ends, behaviour, waste and appetite.

Weekly

Feed according to age and species; follow calcium/vitamin rhythm; clean bowls and high-contact surfaces.

Monthly

Deep-clean selected areas, inspect heat/UVB equipment, weigh animals where relevant and review enclosure wear.

Seasonal

Adjust photoperiod carefully, evaluate night drops, breeding plans and brumation only for suitable species.
