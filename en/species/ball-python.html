Detailed care sections

Read-more links now scroll to these visible sections.

Enclosure size

Minimum: 120 × 60 × 60 cm for voksen. Recommended: 120–150 × 60 × 60 cm eller større med skjul.

Day/night heat

Day: Varm side 31–33°C; generel gradient 25–30°C. Night: 24–26°C; undgå store kolde fald. Basking/warm zone: 31–33°C varm skjuleplads. Cool zone: 24–26°C.

Light, UVB and photoperiod

Photoperiod: 10–12 timer. UVB kan bruges som lav/moderat berigelse. UVB: Lav/moderat UVB valgfrit med skjul.

Humidity

50–60% normalt; hæv midlertidigt ved hamskifte.

Feeding

Optøede rotter/mus i korrekt størrelse; fodringsinterval efter alder og kropskondition.

Calcium and vitamins

Hele byttedyr kræver normalt ikke tilskud.

Brumation / winter rest

Ingen egentlig vinterdvale i almindeligt hold.

Breeding guidance

Videregående; kræver vægt, alder, kønssikkerhed og plan for æg/unger.

Care, cleaning and supplement plan

This replaces the old static checklist with an actual routine.

Daily

Check water, temperatures at both ends, behaviour, waste and appetite.

Weekly

Feed according to age and species; follow calcium/vitamin rhythm; clean bowls and high-contact surfaces.

Monthly

Deep-clean selected areas, inspect heat/UVB equipment, weigh animals where relevant and review enclosure wear.

Seasonal

Adjust photoperiod carefully, evaluate night drops, breeding plans and brumation only for suitable species.
