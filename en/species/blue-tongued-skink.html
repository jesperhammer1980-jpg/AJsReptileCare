Detailed care sections

Read-more links now scroll to these visible sections.

Enclosure size

Minimum: 120 × 60 × 60 cm. Recommended: 150 × 60 × 60 cm eller større.

Day/night heat

Day: Varm side 32–35°C; generelt 24–30°C. Night: 18–22°C. Basking/warm zone: 35–40°C overflade afhængigt af art. Cool zone: 22–25°C.

Light, UVB and photoperiod

Photoperiod: 12 timer; tydelig dag/nat-rytme. UVB: Moderat UVB anbefales.

Humidity

40–60% for nordlige typer; højere for indonesiske typer.

Feeding

Omnivor: grønt, grøntsager, snegle/insekter og kontrolleret protein.

Calcium and vitamins

Calcium og multivitamin efter kost; D3 afhænger af UVB.

Brumation / winter rest

Nogle australske former kan have sæsonhvile; afhænger af art.

Breeding guidance

Kræver arts-/underartsviden; levende unger og behov for plads.

Care, cleaning and supplement plan

Daily, weekly and seasonal routines for stable care.

Daily

Check water, temperatures at both ends, behaviour, waste and appetite.

Weekly

Feed according to age and species; follow calcium/vitamin rhythm; clean bowls and high-contact surfaces.

Monthly

Deep-clean selected areas, inspect heat/UVB equipment, weigh animals where relevant and review enclosure wear.

Seasonal

Adjust photoperiod carefully, evaluate night drops, breeding plans and brumation only for suitable species.
