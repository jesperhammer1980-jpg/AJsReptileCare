Detailed care sections

Read-more links now scroll to these visible sections.

Enclosure size

Minimum: Stor akvatisk tank/dam; typisk 300+ liter for voksen afhængigt af størrelse. Recommended: Stor dam eller meget rummelig tank med kraftig filtrering og landdel.

Day/night heat

Day: Vand ca. 22–26°C for voksne. Night: 20–24°C vand/rum. Basking/warm zone: 30–35°C tør solplads. Cool zone: Vandzone 22–26°C.

Light, UVB and photoperiod

Photoperiod: 10–12 timer. UVB: Stærk UVB over tør solplads plus varme.

Humidity

Akvatisk art; vandkvalitet er vigtigere end luftfugtighed.

Feeding

Varieret akvatisk kost; voksne mere plantebaseret, unge mere animalsk.

Calcium and vitamins

Calciumkilder og UVB er vigtige for skjold/skelet.

Brumation / winter rest

Kan brumere naturligt, men bør ikke forsøges uden stor erfaring og lovlig/sikker opsætning.

Breeding guidance

Ikke anbefalet; arten er invasiv/reguleret mange steder. Kræver plan for æg og lovgivning.

Care, cleaning and supplement plan

Daily, weekly and seasonal routines for stable care.

Daily

Check water, temperatures at both ends, behaviour, waste and appetite.

Weekly

Feed according to age and species; follow calcium/vitamin rhythm; clean bowls and high-contact surfaces.

Monthly

Deep-clean selected areas, inspect heat/UVB equipment, weigh animals where relevant and review enclosure wear.

Seasonal

Adjust photoperiod carefully, evaluate night drops, breeding plans and brumation only for suitable species.
