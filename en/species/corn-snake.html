Enclosure size

Minimum: 120 × 60 × 60 cm for voksen. Recommended: 120–150 × 60 × 60 cm med klatremuligheder.

Day/night heat

Day: Varm side 28–30°C; omgivende 24–28°C. Night: 18–22°C. Basking/warm zone: 28–30°C varm zone. Cool zone: 22–24°C.

Light, UVB and photoperiod

Photoperiod: 10–12 timer naturlig døgnrytme. UVB: Lav UVB kan bruges som berigelse, men arten skal have skjul.

Humidity

40–50%, lidt højere ved hamskifte.

Feeding: thawed, fresh-killed or live prey?

Snakes may be fed properly thawed frozen prey, fresh-killed prey, or in special cases live prey. The choice depends on species, previous feeding habits, feeding response, local rules and keeper experience.

Thawed/frozen prey
  • Practical to store and size correctly.
  • Reduces the risk of bite injuries to the snake.
  • Requires correct thawing, warming and hygiene.
  • Some individuals need to be transitioned to it.
Live prey
  • May trigger a stronger hunting and feeding response in some animals.
  • Can be relevant in special cases with difficult feeders.
  • Requires constant supervision and correct prey size.
  • Risk of bite injuries and stress inside the enclosure.
Practical line

For most hobby keepers, thawed or fresh-killed prey is the most controllable option. Live feeding should only be handled with experience, supervision and awareness of local rules.

Care, cleaning and supplement plan

Daily, weekly and seasonal routines for stable care.

Daily

Check water, temperatures at both ends, behaviour, waste and appetite.

Weekly

Feed according to age and species; follow calcium/vitamin rhythm; clean bowls and high-contact surfaces.

Monthly

Deep-clean selected areas, inspect heat/UVB equipment, weigh animals where relevant and review enclosure wear.

Seasonal

Adjust photoperiod carefully, evaluate night drops, breeding plans and brumation only for suitable species.
