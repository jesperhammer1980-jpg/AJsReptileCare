Care plan, not a clumsy checklist

Species pages now use a daily/weekly/monthly/seasonal plan.

Daily

Water, temperatures, behaviour, waste and obvious health signs.

Weekly

Feeding rhythm, supplement rhythm, spot-cleaning and humidity checks.

Monthly

Deep clean, equipment audit, body condition and UVB age check.

Seasonal

Photoperiod, night drop, breeding decisions and brumation where relevant.

AJ’s Reptile Care

Care ranges are conservative guide values. Use thermostats, digital thermometers and qualified reptile veterinary advice for animal-specific decisions.
