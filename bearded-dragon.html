What should you check first?

  • Thermal gradient: warm side, cool side and basking area must be measured separately.
  • UVB and day/night rhythm must match the species natural activity.
  • Humidity is measured with a hygrometer and adjusted with ventilation, water bowl, misting or humid hides.
